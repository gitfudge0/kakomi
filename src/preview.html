Capture help

Start the picker with the extension icon or Alt + Shift + S. Use ↑ to select a parent, ↓ to return to a child, and Esc to exit. Click or press Enter to capture an element, or drag a rectangle and release to capture an area. Choose clipboard and preview behavior in Settings.

Captures include the highlighted visible rectangle, including any overlapping page content. Offscreen content is excluded. Frames and closed shadow roots are selectable as whole elements. Browser settings, the Chrome Web Store, and other protected pages cannot be selected.

Previews are held temporarily in browser memory and may expire as new captures are made. Download anything you want to keep.
