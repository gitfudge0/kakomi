SETTINGS

After the screenshot.

Choose where your captures go. Changes save automatically.

Capture destinations

Keep at least one destination enabled. If automatic copying fails, the preview opens so you can copy or download your screenshot.

Two ways to capture

Click the extension icon, then click a highlighted element, or click and drag to select a custom rectangle. Release to capture. Use ↑ / ↓ for parent and child elements, and Esc to cancel.
